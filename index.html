Shiv Sena leader warns comedian Kunal Kamra over Eknath Shinde joke: ‘Will make sure you don't move freely’

Stand-up comedian Kunal Kamra has landed in a fresh soup due to his alleged joke on Shiv Sena leader and Maharashtra deputy chief minister Eknath Shinde, with party leaders threatening him of "dire consequences".

Kunal Kamra's show, which was filmed at Hotel Unicontinental in Mumbai's Khar, featured a 'gaddar' (traitor) jibe at Eknath Shinde. Without mentioning any names, he seemingly mocked the deputy CM by modifying a song from the movie ‘Dil Toh Pagal Hai’.

Canada set for snap polls on April 28 as House of Commons dissolved

Toronto: Canada is set for a snap election on April 28. Prime Minister Mark Carney met the Governor General Mary Simon on Sunday morning in Ottawa and requested her to dissolve the House of Commons, precipitating the campaign to form the country’s next government.
